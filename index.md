## The problem

There are some people who have a passion for basketball and want to explore new courts. When looking for courts on a regular map application, they are not given information about who is there, the condition of the court, which courts are present and recent news about the area.

## The Solution

This project provides a database for these passionate basketball players to use when finding a court and people to play basketball with. There they will get the answers they are looking for when finding their dream place to play. 

## Approach

Hoops Hawaii will only be comprised of one role: user.
